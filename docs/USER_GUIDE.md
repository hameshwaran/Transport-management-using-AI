# FleetAI User Guide

Welcome to **FleetAI**, your command center for predictive transport maintenance. This guide will help you navigate and utilize the core features of the platform.

## Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
2. Select your role (Master Admin, Fleet Manager, or Maintenance Tech) on the Sign-In page.
3. Use the default password "password" to authorize access.

## Dashboard Overview
The Dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of your fleet:
- **Active Vehicles**: Total count of operational trucks and vans.
- **Critical Alerts**: Immediate attention required.
- **AI Recommended Actions**: A prioritized list of maintenance tasks generated by the AI engine. Click **Schedule** to instantly move a recommendation to the master timeline.

## Managing the Fleet
Navigate to the **Fleet** tab to view the detailed status of every vehicle:
- **Health Bars**: Color-coded indicators showing component longevity.
- **View Telematics**: Click any vehicle card to see live simulated sensor data (Engine Temp, Oil Pressure, etc.).
- **Add Vehicle**: Use the button at the top right to register new assets into the system.

## Maintenance Scheduling
The **Schedule** tab shows a chronological timeline of upcoming services:
- **Predictive vs Routine**: The system distinguishes between AI-predicted failures and standard scheduled maintenance.
- **Timeline Tracking**: Monitor progress from "Scheduled" to "In Progress" and "Completed."

## System Alerts
The **Alerts** tab is your emergency hub:
- **Fix Now**: Clicking this simulates a successful repair, restoring the vehicle's health to 100% and updating the system dashboard instantly.

## Settings & Administration
- **Notification Preferences**: Toggle email and push alert sensitivity.
- **AI Thresholds**: Adjust the "Critical Warning" sliders to re-calibrate how the AI identifies risks.
- **Manage Team**: Create new administrative roles for your staff.
- **Data Management**: Export your entire fleet status to a **CSV file** for external reporting.
